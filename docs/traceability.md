# Traceability: user stories to the tests that prove them (R1)

- **Status:** written 2026-11-24 with the release of R1 (Prompt 8.3). Every user story of `docs/requirements.md`
  section 4, US-01 to US-44, has a row, and every row names the tests that prove its acceptance criteria.
  `TraceabilityTests` (tests/BillingRecon.ArchitectureTests) fails when a story has no row, a row has no test, or a test
  a row names no longer exists; a new story or a renamed test is therefore caught by the build.

A test is named as `path#name`: the file, from the repository root, and the C# test method or the title of the frontend
test (Vitest in `frontend/src`, Playwright in `frontend/e2e`). The tests on SQL Server are those under
`tests/BillingRecon.IntegrationTests/Database`; they run with Docker (`docs/runbook.md` section 4).

Three things hold for every story and are not repeated in its row:

- **No drift after any scenario** (BR-404, US-41): `DatabaseTest` runs the reconciliation of every tenant after every
  test of the `Database` collection and fails on any drift row, gapless numbering included.
- **Every endpoint answers each role as the permission matrix says** (`PermissionMatrixApiTests`, ADR 0032).
- **Every state change is audited** (BR-701): each new use case has a test that counts its audit record (ADR 0020).

Where a clause is proven by a test whose name does not say so, or only partly, the notes after the table say how.

## Notes

- **US-02, a disabled user is refused:** a case (`SignInRefusal.UserDisabled`) of the `BR601_every_refused_sign_in`
  theory.
- **US-12, no new payments for an inactive payer:**
  `PaymentsApiTests#A_payment_that_is_refused_takes_no_number_and_writes_nothing` expects 422 `payer_inactive`.
  **Balances still reported** after a deactivation has no test of its own: the reports read balances without regard to
  the payer's status, and `PaymentAllocationsApiTests#A_payment_of_a_payer_who_was_deactivated_since_can_still_be_allocated`
  shows an inactive payer's money still moves (`docs/backlog.md`).
- **US-21, one transaction (BR-007):** proven with US-25 by
  `BillPostingApiTests#US25_a_failure_at_any_step_leaves_no_number_no_ledger_and_no_audit_rows_and_the_next_posting_gets_the_number`
  and its sibling tests.
- **US-33, refused when a refund exists:** `RefundsApiTests` (the US-34 scenario ends on 422 `payment_refunded`),
  `RefundHandlerTests` and the frontend's refusal test. The reversal is tested with the reason of a dishonoured cheque;
  the method of the payment does not change what is proven.
- **US-36:** proven by the tests of stale versions and the `BR804_` races, a financial POST among them.
- **US-41, zero drift after every scenario:** the after-test check of `DatabaseTest` (above); the named tests prove a
  seeded drift is reported, logged and kept to its hospital.
- **US-42, the business date in India:** a payment at 18:45 UTC belongs to the next day in India
  (`PaymentsApiTests`), and the dashboard's and the collection's day boundaries are tested at 23:59 and 00:01 in India.
- **US-44:** the row names six of its tests; the trend and the four panels have more in `DashboardApiTests` and
  `Dashboard.test.tsx`. A BillingClerk's and a Cashier's 403 are rows of `PermissionMatrixApiTests`.
- **US-01:** no screen manages users in R1 (no prompt builds it), so its proof is the API's, the Domain's and the
  Application's tests.
